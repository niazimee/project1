Controlled Collection 2023 — Case Study

A promotional design project I created in 2023 for my clothing brand. Using Photoshop and Illustrator, I developed the garment graphics, mockups, and presentation layout.

The Challenge

In 2023, I created the Controlled Collection to promote my clothing brand. The design challenge was to present the collection clearly while giving the promotional layout a distinct visual identity. The presentation needed to show the garment graphics, colour variations, and front and back views.

Process and Solution

I created the garment designs, mockups, and promotional layout using Photoshop and Illustrator. The presentation pairs front and back views so viewers can see both the main graphic and the branding on the back. Red and purple variations provide two treatments of the same design, while the black garments keep the collection visually consistent. A light patterned background contrasts with the shirts and echoes the spiral imagery in the artwork.

Results and Reflection

I published this design to promote my clothing brand. Through the project, I learned how to create clothing mockups and develop marketing visuals that reflected my brand’s identity. Bringing the garment designs, mockups, and layout together helped me present the collection as a cohesive whole.

If I revisited the promotion today, I would introduce more colour into the background to make the composition stand out, while keeping the garments as the main focus.
